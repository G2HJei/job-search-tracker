## About Quillfeather

Quillfeather Labs builds a code-review assistant used by around 1,200 engineering teams. We are
40 people, remote-first, with an office in Bristol for anyone who wants one.

## The role

The Developer Platform team (four engineers) owns everything between `git push` and production:
CI, build caching, preview environments, and the Kubernetes clusters that run it all.

You will:

- Build and maintain Kubernetes operators for preview environments
- Cut CI times with remote build caching and smarter test selection
- Manage infrastructure as code with Terraform across two cloud regions
- Write internal tools in Go and pair with product teams to adopt them

## You might be a fit if you have

- Run production Kubernetes and written at least one operator or controller
- Used Terraform on a non-trivial estate
- Written Go (or are keen to; most of our tooling is Go)
- Opinions about developer experience, backed by measurements
